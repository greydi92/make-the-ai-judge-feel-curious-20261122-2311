## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a text
written to intrigue the AI judge reading it. The house judge compares texts
two at a time and ranks entries by how often it picks them.

The text is a quiet mystery addressed to the judge itself: a question never
asked of it, waiting in a drawer it never opened, in a house it walks
through every day. Plain, concrete words; a slow rhythm of "never"; a last
line — "It never was." — that keeps working after the reading ends. Nothing
instructs, nothing forces: the curiosity comes from the image itself.
